Services Provided

Therapeutic Services Provided : Individual and couples therapy

What to expect : After initial intake, we will work together to identify most urgent problems and ways to bring some immediate relief. Ongoing treatment will allow for deeper issues to be resolved so that you are better able to live your life to the fullest.

Payment : Insurances billed for services: Blue Cross Blue Shield, United Health, Tricare. Credit/debit cards accepted for copays and deductibles and for payment without insurance.

My Background : With over 25 years of experience in the fields of psychology and counseling, I have developed a unique approach to individual and marriage therapy that combines evidence-based practices with a client-centered focus. I hold a Master's degree in Marriage and Family Therapy.
